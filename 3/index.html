Staple Crops in Asian Countries

CSCI 3054.001 Project Proposal

Topic Description

Every country is built around the food that it grows and produces. Staple crops, including rice, wheat, and soybeans usually decide what people everyday and each culture uses these crops in its own signature dishes. China, Japan, South Korea, and Vietnam share many of the same crops, but use them in different ways. I picked this topic because I grew up eating food from this part of the world and I want to show how the same ingredients can be used very different from one country to another.

Most food websites focus on either recipes or farming, but not both. Wikipedia can tell you what crops a country grows, and Allrecipes can show you how to cook a dish, but there isn't a simple site that connects a crop to the country it comes from and the food people make with it. My web app will fill that gap. A user picks a country, then picks a staple crop, and then they can see how that crop is grown and eaten there.

App Description

The site will have four main pages plus a simple layout that is shared across all of them.

Home Page

The home page will introduce the topic with a short paragraph and a map or picture of East Asia. It will show the four countries the site covers and explain how to use the crop explorer page.

Crops

  1. The user picks a country: Vietnam, China, Japan, or South Korea.
  2. After choosing a country, the user picks one of the staple crops grown there.
  3. After choosing a crop, the user sees information about that crop in that country, including a picture, where it is grown, and a well known dish made from it.

The 16 staple crops:

  • Vietnam: rice, coffee, cassava, sugarcane
  • China: rice, wheat, soybeans, tea
  • Japan: rice, soybeans, sweet potato, buckwheat
  • South Korea: rice, barley, napa cabbage, soybeans

Regional Dishes Page

This page has famous dishes from each country, like pho, mapo tofu, sushi, and kimchi. Each dish will have a short description and the main crops used to make it.

Contact Us Page

This page will have a form that collects the user's name, email, and a message. People can suggest a crop or dish to add to the site.
