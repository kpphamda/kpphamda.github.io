Welcome

Staple crops are ones that are grown in large quantities and are the most eaten by people every day. China, Japan, South Korea, and Vietnam share many of the same crops but use them in very different ways. This site connects each crop to the country it comes from and the food people make with it.

Countries covered

  • Vietnam
  • China
  • Japan
  • South Korea

How to use the crop explorer

  1. Go to the Crops page
  2. Pick a country
  3. Pick a staple crop grown there
  4. A picture, where it is grown, and a popular dish made from it will be shown
