Monkey D Luffy

Monkey D. Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates in One Piece. He ate the gum gum fruit and became a rubber man Now he is on a quest to find the One Piece and become the Pirate King.

  • He decides what he wants and never fails to follow through.
  • He treats his crew like family and will fight an entire government to get one of them back.
  • He just wants everyone to have JOY.
